Alerts

Every alert shows on the Dashboard. Add an email address or a WhatsApp number to be sent them as well. Which price changes raise an alert is chosen per hunt, in the hunt's Alerts panel.

Email

Gmail will not let an app sign in with your normal password. Create an app password at myaccount.google.com/apppasswords (2-Step Verification has to be on) and paste it here.

WhatsApp

WhatsApp

WhatsApp messages go through the free CallMeBot service. To get a key, follow its three steps: you message its bot from this number and it replies with the key.
