A search opens every store of every active hunt, so it is the heaviest thing the tracker does; once a day is plenty. Checking every hour means more visits to each store, which makes it likelier a store starts refusing the tracker for a while. Six hours is a safe default.

Alerts

Every alert shows on the Dashboard. Add a Discord webhook, an email address or a Telegram bot to be sent them as well. Which price changes raise an alert is chosen per hunt, in the hunt's Alerts panel.

Discord

In Discord: the channel's Edit Channel > Integrations > Webhooks > New Webhook > Copy Webhook URL. Anyone with the link can post to that channel, so treat it like a password.

Email

Email

Gmail will not let an app sign in with your normal password. Create an app password at myaccount.google.com/apppasswords (2-Step Verification has to be on) and paste it here.

Telegram

Telegram

Alerts come from a bot of your own. In Telegram, message @BotFather, send /newbot and answer its two questions; it replies with a token. Then open your new bot and send it any message. Paste the token here and press Save: the tracker finds the chat by itself.
